Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a paid plan to test the API?

No. The Free plan gives you full access to test mode with all API endpoints, entity types, and states. You only need a paid plan when you're ready to submit real formations.

Are state filing fees included in the platform fee?

No, state fees are separate and charged at cost. We pass through the exact fee charged by each state with zero markup. Your total cost = platform fee + state fee.

Can I white-label the formation service?

Yes. On Growth and Enterprise plans, all documents and communications can be branded with your company's identity. Your customers never see our brand.

What happens if I exceed my plan's formation limit?

We won't cut you off. Overages are billed at a per-formation rate: $5/formation on Starter, $3/formation on Growth. Enterprise plans have no limits.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. Annual billing gets a 20% discount. Enterprise plans can be customized with annual contracts.
