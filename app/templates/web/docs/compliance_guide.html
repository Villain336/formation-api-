Compliance Guide

This guide covers the legal and regulatory requirements for offering business formation services through the Formation API. Understanding these requirements is essential for operating a compliant formation platform.

Disclaimer: This guide is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Consult with a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction for specific legal requirements applicable to your business.

Who Needs to Read This

If you're building a platform that offers business formation to end customers, you need to understand:

  • What you're legally allowed to do (and what requires a law license)
  • How to properly disclose your services
  • What data you must collect and protect
  • Ongoing compliance obligations for formed entities

Legal Framework for Formation Services

What Is and Isn't "Practicing Law"

Business formation services generally fall into two categories:

Permitted (Non-Legal)Requires Attorney
Filing state formation documentsProviding legal advice on entity selection
Transmitting documents to the stateDrafting custom legal documents
Providing factual information about requirementsInterpreting laws for specific situations
Generating standard-form documents from templatesRepresenting clients before state agencies
Tracking compliance deadlinesTax planning and structuring advice
Formation API provides: Document filing, template-based document generation, factual state requirement data, and compliance tracking. We do not provide legal advice. Your platform should include appropriate disclaimers.

Required Disclosures

Your platform must clearly disclose:

  1. "We are not a law firm." — Required in most states. Clearly state that you provide document filing services, not legal advice.
  2. "This is not legal advice." — On all pages where formation information is presented.
  3. Fee transparency — Clearly break down state fees vs. service fees. Never bundle them deceptively.
  4. Processing times — State actual state processing times. Don't promise faster than the state can deliver.
  5. Refund policy — State filing fees are non-refundable once submitted. Your service fee refund policy must be clearly stated.

Data Collection Requirements

What You Must Collect

State formation filings require specific information. The Formation API validates these per-state:

  • Entity name (must comply with state naming rules)
  • Registered agent name and address (required in all states)
  • Principal office address
  • Member/manager/director names and addresses
  • Organizer/incorporator information
  • Business purpose (some states require specific language)

For EIN Applications

  • Responsible party name
  • Responsible party SSN or ITIN (we only store last 4 digits)
  • Entity name and type
  • Formation state and date

Data Protection Requirements

  • SSN/ITIN: Collect only the minimum necessary (last 4 digits). Never store full SSNs unless absolutely required. Encrypt at rest.
  • PII: Names, addresses, phone numbers, emails must be stored securely with access controls.
  • Financial data: Use Stripe for all payment processing. Never handle credit card numbers directly.
  • Retention: Keep formation records for at least 7 years (IRS requirement). Allow users to request data deletion for non-essential data.

FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI)

Since January 2024, most newly formed entities must file a BOI report with FinCEN within 90 days of formation.

What Your Platform Should Do

  1. Inform customers about the BOI requirement at the time of formation.
  2. Track the deadline — Formation API automatically creates a compliance task.
  3. Provide filing assistance or direct customers to the FinCEN filing system.

BOI Exemptions

Not all entities need to file. Exempt entities include: publicly traded companies, entities with 20+ employees and $5M+ revenue, banks, credit unions, and certain regulated entities.

Annual Report Requirements

Most states require annual or biennial reports. Failure to file can result in:

  • Late fees and penalties
  • Administrative dissolution of the entity
  • Loss of good standing status
  • Personal liability for members/directors

Formation API automatically creates compliance tasks with due dates for each state's annual report requirements.

Operating as a Registered Agent

If you offer registered agent services through the Formation API:

  • Physical presence: A registered agent must have a physical address (not PO Box) in each state where they serve.
  • Availability: Must be available during normal business hours to accept service of process.
  • Notification: Must promptly forward all received documents to the entity.
  • State registration: Some states require registered agents to register with the state before serving.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Considerations

While business formation services are not directly subject to the Bank Secrecy Act, the Corporate Transparency Act imposes related obligations:

  • Know Your Customer (KYC): Consider implementing identity verification for formation customers, especially for high-risk states or entity types.
  • Suspicious Activity: Be alert to unusual patterns (bulk formations, shell company indicators).
  • Record Keeping: Maintain records of who ordered each formation and their relationship to the entity.

Recommended Disclaimers

Include these disclaimers on your platform:

// On formation pages:
"[Your Company] is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
We provide business formation document filing services. For legal
advice regarding entity selection, tax implications, or business
structuring, please consult a licensed attorney or tax professional."

// On pricing pages:
"State filing fees are set by each state's Secretary of State and
are subject to change. Our service fee is separate from and in
addition to the state filing fee. State fees are non-refundable
once the filing has been submitted."

// On EIN application pages:
"EIN applications are submitted to the Internal Revenue Service (IRS).
Processing times are determined by the IRS and may vary. We do not
guarantee specific processing times for EIN applications."

Checklist for Launch

  • Terms of Service clearly define your role as a filing service, not a law firm
  • Privacy Policy covers collection and use of PII, SSN data, and financial information
  • "Not a law firm" disclaimer on all formation-related pages
  • Fee breakdown clearly separates state fees from service fees
  • Processing time estimates match actual state processing times
  • Refund policy clearly stated and accessible
  • Data encryption at rest and in transit implemented
  • SSN data minimized (last 4 only) and encrypted
  • Payment processing handled through PCI-compliant provider (Stripe)
  • BOI reporting requirement communicated to customers
  • Compliance deadline tracking active for all completed formations
  • State-specific publication requirements flagged where applicable
