Regulatory Compliance

How Formation API meets legal and regulatory requirements for business formation services.

Our Compliance Approach

Business formation involves regulated activities across 50 US states plus federal requirements. We maintain compliance through a combination of legal review, technology controls, and operational procedures.

State Formation Compliance

Filing Accuracy

  • Formation documents are generated from state-approved templates reviewed by attorneys in each jurisdiction
  • State naming rules (required suffixes, restricted words) are validated before submission
  • Required fields are enforced per state requirements
  • Filing fees are set to the exact amount published by each state's Secretary of State

State-Specific Requirements

  • Publication states (NY, AZ, NE, PA): We flag publication requirements and create compliance tracking tasks automatically
  • Initial report states: Automatic reminders for states requiring initial reports within a specific timeframe
  • Registered agent requirements: All states require a registered agent. We validate this requirement is met for every formation

Federal Compliance

FinCEN Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI)

  • Compliance tasks for BOI filing are automatically created for all new formations
  • 90-day deadline tracking from formation date
  • Customer notifications before the deadline

IRS Requirements

  • EIN applications are submitted in compliance with IRS procedures
  • We store only the last 4 digits of SSN/ITIN as required for EIN applications
  • Full SSN is transmitted to the IRS only during the application process and is not retained

Data Protection Compliance

CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

  • Right to know: Users can request a copy of all personal data we hold
  • Right to delete: Users can request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • No sale of personal information: We never sell personal data
  • Non-discrimination: No service degradation for exercising privacy rights

Data Minimization

  • We collect only the data necessary for formation filings
  • SSN data is limited to last 4 digits and encrypted
  • Payment data is processed entirely by Stripe
  • Data retention follows legal minimums

Payment Compliance

  • PCI DSS: Payment processing through Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1). We qualify as SAQ-A (no cardholder data touches our systems)
  • Fee transparency: State fees are itemized separately from service fees. No hidden markups.
  • Refund policy: Clearly stated. State fees non-refundable once submitted; service fees refundable for unsubmitted orders.

Not a Law Firm Disclosure

Formation API, Inc. is a technology company that provides business formation document filing services. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. This is disclosed:

  • In our Terms of Service
  • On all formation-related pages
  • In API response metadata where applicable
  • In generated documents where appropriate

Audit and Reporting

  • SOC 2 Type II audit in progress (available for Enterprise customers upon request)
  • Annual security assessments by third-party firms
  • Automated compliance monitoring for all active formations
  • Monthly compliance reporting available for Enterprise customers
